About

I'm a Computer Engineering student at Simon Fraser University. Right now I write firmware for the SFU Robot Soccer team, developing real-time embedded C on STM32 microcontrollers that drives the motors and radio links of autonomous RoboCup robots.

Before that, I led the mechanical design of an automated measuring cup with an 8-person team, from CAD modeling and stress analysis to a motor-driven dispensing mechanism tested across 2 prototypes. I like work that sits where code, circuits, and mechanical parts meet.
